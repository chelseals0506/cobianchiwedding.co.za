Dress Code

Forest Formal — elegant but comfortable for an outdoor setting.

  • Ladies: cocktail or midi dresses in neutrals, florals, champagne, beige, navy or soft pastels.
  • Gents: suits or chinos with blazers. Browns, tan, navy and charcoal work beautifully.
  • Please avoid wearing green (reserved for the bridal party) and white/ivory.
  • Bring a shawl/jacket for the evening chill.

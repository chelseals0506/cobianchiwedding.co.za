Our Story

Chelsea‑Lea and Gino's story began back in 2016, when two high school students crossed paths and sparked a connection that would change their lives forever. What started as friendship soon grew into something deeper, and by 2017, these high school sweethearts were officially inseparable. From navigating the ups and downs of teenage years to building a life filled with shared dreams and laughter, their love has only grown stronger with time. After nearly a decade together, they are so excited to celebrate their wedding day surrounded by the people who have supported and loved them along the way and begin their next chapter as husband and wife.

Dress Code

- Ladies: Midi or maxi dresses, flowy styles, florals or soft earthy tones or pastel. Block heels, wedges or flats are best as we’ll be on grass and natural paths. - Gentlemen: A suit, or a blazer with chinos/dress pants and a button-up shirt. Tie optional. Earthy colours like beige, tan, brown, navy or olive will suit the setting beautifully.

Please avoid green (reserved for bridal party) and white/ivory.
